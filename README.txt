This file contains the experimental results of the sessile droplet analysis for each sequence. OP indentifyes the OpenDrop results, the rest of the columns are from the FD method

Columns:
OP_theta (deg): Rotation angle finded by OpenDrop for the axis of symmetry of the profile in deegres.
R0 (mm): Mean radius of the apex raduis in millimeters
OP_Bo: Mean Bond number of the droplet with respect the apex
OP_S_Bo: Standard deviation of the Bond number
OP_gamma (N/m): Mean surface tension of the droplet in N/m
OP_S_gamma (N/m): Standard deviation (repeatibility) of the surface tension in N/m
d (mm): Mean diameter of the droplet in millimeters
B_FD: Mean Bond number from the FD method
S_B_FD: Standard deviation of the Bond number from the FD method
<Sigma_B>_FD: Mean standard deviation of the slope (B) from the linear fit with the FD method
Gamma_FD (N/m): Mean surface tension of the droplet in N/m from the FD method
S_Gama_FD (N/m): Standard deviation of the surface tension in N/m from the FD method
Shape_parameter: Mean shape parameter of the droplet
Contact_Angle (deg): Mean contact angle of the droplet in degrees
Volume (uL): Mean volume of the droplet in microliters
Area (mm^2): Mean area of the droplet in square millimeters
Neumann_number: Mean Neumann number of the droplet
Worthington_number: Mean Worthington number of the droplet
Fitted_points: Mean number of points used for the fit (integer value)
